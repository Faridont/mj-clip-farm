MJ Clip Farm

A private desktop tool for the @marmok_and_johan fan clip accounts.

MJ Clip Farm runs on a single computer owned by the account owner. It turns long gaming videos into short vertical clips with subtitles and publishes one clip per day to our own accounts:

  • TikTok — @marmok_and_johan
  • Instagram — @marmok_and_johan
  • YouTube — @marmok_and_johan

The tool is not offered to the public. It only signs in to the accounts listed above and posts content to them; it does not read, collect or post to anyone else's account.

How it works

  1. The account owner signs in once with the platform's official login (for TikTok — Login Kit).
  2. The tool prepares a clip, a caption and hashtags.
  3. The clip is uploaded through the official Content Posting API to the owner's account.
