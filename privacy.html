Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

MJ Clip Farm ("the app") is a private desktop tool used by the owner of the @marmok_and_johan accounts to publish short video clips to those accounts. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by the owner of the @marmok_and_johan accounts. It is not available to the public and does not have user registration.

Data we access

  • Basic profile information of the owner's own account (such as account ID and display name), used only to confirm that the app is connected to the correct account.
  • Access tokens issued by the platform after the owner signs in. They are used only to upload videos to the owner's account.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not access, collect or store data about other users, followers, comments or messages.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in a local configuration file that is not published or transmitted anywhere except to the platform's own API when uploading a video. The owner can revoke access at any time in the platform's account settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions).

Data deletion

Revoking the app's access in the platform settings makes the stored tokens useless. Deleting the app's local configuration file removes all stored tokens from the computer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the @marmok_and_johan account.
